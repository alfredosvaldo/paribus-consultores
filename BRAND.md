# PARIBUS — BRAND SYSTEM

## Core

**Brand:** PARIBUS  
**Descriptor:** Consultoría económica, financiera y regulatoria  
**Tagline:** Rigor para decisiones complejas.  
**Concept:** Aislar lo que importa.

## Positioning

Paribus es una consultora boutique que combina análisis económico, financiero y regulatorio con criterio senior para apoyar decisiones complejas.

## Personality

- rigurosa
- precisa
- sobria
- intelectual
- discreta
- senior
- contemporánea
- independiente

## Never

- ostentosa
- startup-like
- fintech-like
- legal-firm cosplay
- generic corporate
- over-designed
- overly academic

## Visual direction

Editorial financiero + arquitectura contemporánea + consultoría boutique.

### Palette

- Espresso 900: `#29211C`
- Charcoal 950: `#171614`
- Ivory 050: `#F3EFE8`
- Stone 300: `#B7AA9E`
- Copper 500: `#A66F46`
- Mineral 700: `#47362C`
- Warm White: `#FAF8F4`

### Typography

- Display: Instrument Serif
- UI/body: Manrope

### Logo

Prefer wordmark PARIBUS. Minimal geometric intervention only.

Never use scales, columns, arrows, money symbols, stock charts, handshakes, buildings, AI brains or circuits.

## Motion

Slow, deliberate, precise, quiet.

Use motion to reveal structure from complexity.

Avoid typing effects, bouncy easing, excessive parallax, scroll hijacking, cursor gimmicks and gratuitous 3D.

## Imagery

Preferred:
- abstract geometry
- material studies
- smoked glass
- dark wood
- copper
- stone
- editorial portraiture

Avoid:
- office stock
- handshakes
- generic executives
- trading screens
- city skylines as primary imagery
